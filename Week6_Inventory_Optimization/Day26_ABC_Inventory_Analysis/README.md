\# Day 26 – ABC Inventory Analysis



\## Objective

Classify products based on their sales revenue contribution using the ABC inventory management technique.



\## Implementation

\- Analysed the latest 28 days of M5 sales history.

\- Combined sales quantities with weekly selling prices.

\- Calculated revenue for each store-product combination.

\- Ranked products by sales value.

\- Applied ABC classification using cumulative revenue contribution.

\- Generated category-level summary reports.



\## Classification

\- A: First 80% of cumulative sales value.

\- B: Next 15%.

\- C: Remaining 5%.



\## Results

\- Total store-product combinations: 144

\- A category: 46 products (80.35% revenue)

\- B category: 47 products (14.67% revenue)

\- C category: 51 products (4.99% revenue)



\## Output Files

\- `day26\_abc\_inventory\_analysis.csv`

\- `day26\_abc\_category\_summary.csv`



\## Testing

Automated tests cover ABC classification, revenue contribution, cumulative percentages, category summaries and zero-value validation.



\## Outcome

Successfully implemented ABC inventory analysis to identify high-value products and prioritize inventory management.
